## Pre-launch legal checklist (very high level)

**P0 — blockers (clear before public launch)**

- [ ] Solicitor sign-off on the Terms of Use + Privacy Policy (hand over the committed review doc as the brief)
- [X] Register with the ICO + pay the data-protection fee (~£52/yr) — DIY, ~10 mins
- [ ] Confirm health-data (special-category) consent meets UK GDPR Art. 9 / Data (Use and Access) Act 2025
- [ ] Confirm the 16+ age gate / Children's Code position is sufficient
- [ ] Documented US data-transfer mechanism (UK–US Data Bridge / IDTA / SCCs) for Anthropic, Perplexity, Railway
- [ ] Resolve Strava API-terms compliance (their terms restrict AI use)

**P1 — before/at launch**

- [ ] Subscriptions: App Store/Play auto-renewal disclosures + UK DMCC subscription rules
- [ ] App Store / Play "data safety" + AI-generated-content disclosures match the policy
- [ ] Fill Etapa Ltd company number + registered office into the published policies
- [ ] Confirm Apple/Google/Tide accounts held by Etapa Ltd (not personally); professional-indemnity insurance
