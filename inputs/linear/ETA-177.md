# Onboarding: ask about existing injuries / conditions to be aware of

Add a question during onboarding (or before generating a plan) asking the user whether they have any existing injuries, conditions, or physical limitations the app should be conscious of when suggesting rides, intensity, or position.

**Why**

* Beginner cyclists often have niggles (knees, lower back, wrists, neck) that affect what's safe and comfortable.
* Surfacing this up front lets us tailor recommendations and avoid pushing intensity/duration that could aggravate something.

**Open questions**

* Free-text field, multi-select from a list, or both?
* Do we store it on the profile and let users update it later in settings?
* Should certain answers nudge specific recommendations (e.g. shorter rides, more rest days, bike-fit reminder)?
* Any disclaimer needed (not medical advice)?
